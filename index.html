    03.5 · Creative test · 3 posts + 1 reel concept

    We chose ROX.

    Because it is the hardest brief of the three.

    Deepest official imagery

    Two models on sale locally — ROX 01 and ROX ADAMAS — give the richest pool of official photography to test craft against.

    The hardest idea to explain

    Range-extender technology is unfamiliar. Making it simple is the real test of creative clarity.

    The highest bar on craft

    A premium audience notices every kerning, crop and cut. If we hold that bar here, we hold it everywhere.

    No prices, offers or specifications are used in the test — only what can be published without further approval.

    Creative test · three post designs

    Three posts, three jobs: desire, understanding, action.

    B · Creative test · reel concept

    “The Quiet Departure”

    30-second reel · ROX ADAMAS · 9:16

    A Cairo family leaves the city before sunrise and reaches the sea — and the loudest thing in the film is the door closing. Silence becomes the proof of electric drive; the open road becomes the proof of range-extender freedom.

    Hook · 0–2 sBlack screen. A door closes — one deep thud. On-screen: “Listen.” Then nothing. The silence makes people stop scrolling.
    Sound directionFoley only for the first 6 seconds (door, tyres, breath). Then a licensed ambient electronic track builds. No voice-over; AR/EN subtitles.
    Closing frameROX logo centred on black, “ROX ADAMAS” beneath, then “Nour Eldin ElSherif Group” credit. Held 2 seconds.
